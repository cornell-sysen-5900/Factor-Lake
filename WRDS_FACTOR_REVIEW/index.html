    WRDS factor matching and default directions

    Review status

    Empirical spreads pending: this catalog contains no real-data results. Run the accompanying script against the authorized WRDS data to complete the evidence.

    Matches are based on documented formula and lookback, not equal vendor values. Units, publication timing and coverage can differ. Similar names, reciprocals and undocumented lookbacks are related candidates only. Unmatched factors are listed, not rebuilt.

    Requested return window: 2002–2026. Tail size: 10%. Source: No data loaded (catalog only)

    Evidence methodology

    Monthly equal-weight tails, 10% by default, with at least one stock per leg and no overlap. Sort finite raw factor values at month end t; use returns in the next calendar month (t+1), including CRSP delisting returns. Break ties by PERMNO. Use the existing buyability filter (positive price and market cap), without a sector or fossil-fuel restriction. No forward-return column is used in selection. For Higher defaults: long high, short low. For Lower defaults: long low, short high. Reported spread is 12 times the arithmetic mean of monthly long-minus-short returns, in percentage points per year; it is NOT a difference of CAGRs and is NOT a compounded trading strategy. Each leg has $1 notional (gross exposure $2); borrowing fees, financing, transaction costs and taxes are excluded. Missing individual returns retain their weight and are filled with 0% as in the app, with a count and coverage flag. Months with fewer than two finite signals, a constant signal, or no next-month return-file coverage are skipped and counted. Actual factor coverage is shown; partial 2002 and 2026 years are not presented as full years. This diagnostic uses raw single-factor ranks, not the app's winsorized multi-factor scoring. Negative default-oriented spreads are marked for team review; no default is flipped automatically. Positive spreads are descriptive evidence, not proof of significance or out-of-sample performance.

    Generate the private evidence report

    Run from the repository root, using your existing local AWS configuration:

    uv run python scripts/wrds_factor_report.py --s3 --output ../private_reports/wrds_factor_review.md

    Or replace --s3 with --data-dir "/path/to/processed/final". The script also writes a monthly CSV and a JSON provenance/summary file beside the report. Keep all real-data outputs outside this public repository. No defaults or data files are modified.
